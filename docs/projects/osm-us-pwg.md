# OSM US PWG

Semi-official resources for the OpenStreetMap US Pedestrian Working Group, including its tagging schema, JOSM tooling, Ultra visualizations, and document archive.

- **Website**: [lumikeiju.dev/osm-us-pwg](https://lumikeiju.dev/osm-us-pwg/)
- **Repository**: [github.com/lumikeiju/osm-us-pwg](https://github.com/lumikeiju/osm-us-pwg)

## Contents

- Machine-readable PWG schema, generated documentation, and schema query tools
- Schema-generated JOSM tagging preset and MapCSS paint style
- JOSM validator rules and Ultra schema-tier and readiness visualizations
- Archived PWG guides, schemas, and templates
