# OpenStreetMap

Collection of OpenStreetMap mapping resources: JOSM presets and validators, MapCSS styles, Ultra visualizations, queries, guides, and icons.

- **Website**: [lumikeiju.dev/openstreetmap](https://lumikeiju.dev/openstreetmap/)
- **Repository**: [github.com/lumikeiju/openstreetmap](https://github.com/lumikeiju/openstreetmap)

## Contents

- JOSM tagging presets, MapCSS paint styles, and validator rules
- Ultra visualizations and Overpass QA queries
- Mapping guides, icons, and light-source illustrations
